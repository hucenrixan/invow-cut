🖨 Print & Cut

1 · Design & print

Keep the design inside the blue page, away from the 3 crosses. In the print window choose Scale 100% and Margins: None. PNG with a transparent background gives the best cut line.

2 · Put cross 1 under the blade

  1. Lift the rollers. Lay the printed sheet printed side up, turned so that cross 1 is at the blade start and cross 2 lies in the direction the head travels across (your cutter: left → right). Cross 3 is then further along the feed.
  2. Slide the sheet until the blade tip is exactly over the centre of cross 1 (green on screen). Keep the sheet edge straight with the guide.
  3. Lower the rollers and press Origin on the cutter.

This cutter can't park the head on a cross from the computer – it always returns to Origin after a job – so cross 1 is lined up by moving the paper.

3 · Check cross 2 (optional, more accurate)

The head goes to cross 2 (blade up) and moves away and back to it 3 times. If the blade tip was not over the cross centre, enter how far the cross was from the blade:

The cross was mm right (− = left)
and mm down (− = up)
